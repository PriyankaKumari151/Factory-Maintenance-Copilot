# Compressor low pressure

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A low-pressure event records pressure below the configured threshold. Potential causes require confirmation by a qualified technician.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Use the approved compressor isolation and depressurisation procedure. A qualified technician should review the regulator and leak inspection record. Do not change operating limits from chatbot guidance.
Related inspection part: P005. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
