# Compressor high temperature

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A temperature event indicates the configured threshold was exceeded. It does not establish a confirmed root cause.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Escalate to the qualified compressor technician. Under the approved isolation and cool-down procedure, review cooling filter condition and maintenance history. Follow the approved restart checklist.
Related inspection part: P006. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
