# Packaging seal failure

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A seal-failure event records inadequate package sealing. Possible factors include material condition and sealing element wear; the event alone does not establish root cause.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Refer the issue to the trained packaging technician. Under the approved isolation procedure, inspect the sealing element and verify settings against the approved product sheet. Do not infer replacement solely from the fault code.
Related inspection part: P003. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
