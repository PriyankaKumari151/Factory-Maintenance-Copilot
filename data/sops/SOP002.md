# Conveyor sensor fault

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A sensor fault indicates missing or inconsistent detection. Inspect the event history before drawing conclusions.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A trained technician should follow the approved isolation procedure and inspect sensor alignment and visible contamination. Record observations before requesting parts.
Related inspection part: P002. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
