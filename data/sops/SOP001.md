# Conveyor belt slip

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A belt-slip event records loss of traction. It does not confirm that the belt requires replacement.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A trained technician must follow the approved isolation procedure before inspecting belt tension and visible wear. Escalate uncertain findings to maintenance.
Related inspection part: P001. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
