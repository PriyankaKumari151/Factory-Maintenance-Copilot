# Packaging film jam

## Fault interpretation
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A film jam records interrupted film movement in the packaging machine. Review the recorded event and product material.

## Technician review
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. A trained technician must use the approved isolation procedure before checking the film path and guide condition. Escalate recurring jams; do not bypass machine guards.
Related inspection part: P004. Listing a part does not recommend replacement.
